- Spec, score, CLI, catalog, teams, skills, routines, patterns

- MCP server that exposes the catalog to Grok Build
- Routine cost estimator (tokens / week) in the spirit of `loop-cost`
- Localization of PROFILE.md (job stays English-first)

Not planned: scraping private share links; unattended production changers; persona packs without jobs.
