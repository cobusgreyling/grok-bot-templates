# Example first run — Research Desk

## Question
Does Grok Bot share one computer across Bots?

## Claims
1. All Bots on an account share one cloud computer (cookies, files, CLI credentials).
   Source: https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/computer-and-apps
2. Separate Bots are not a security boundary.
   Source: https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/approvals-security-and-privacy
3. A share link copies configuration, not computer or logins.
   Source: https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/bots

## Disagreements
None in official docs. Third-party catalogs sometimes imply per-bot isolation — contradicted by the docs above.
